## Komodo DeFi Framework

Wallet, Electrum, peer-to-peer orderbook, and atomic-swap functionality is powered by the [Komodo DeFi Framework](https://github.com/KomodoPlatform/komodo-defi-framework).

Coin and protocol metadata is based on [KomodoPlatform/coins](https://github.com/KomodoPlatform/coins), with Litecoin Cash community compatibility adjustments.
